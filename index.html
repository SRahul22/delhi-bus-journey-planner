Delhi Bus Journey Planner

Bus travel times that follow how Delhi's buses actually move. We turn about 35 million real stop-to-stop bus movements from October 2026 into a traffic model, then serve journey options through a Google-Maps-style API.

How it works

  1. GPS to stop-to-stop times. Each bus ping (about every 10 s) is snapped onto its route's road path. Off-route jumps and duplicates are removed, and the moment each bus passed each stop is interpolated.
  2. Traffic model. For every stretch between two stops: typical and heavy-traffic times by hour of day and weekday/weekend, with fallbacks where data is thin. An XGBoost model corrects whole rides.
  3. Journey planner. A frequency-based RAPTOR router (buses here don't keep to the timetable) finds routes and transfers. Total time = walk + expected wait + ride + transfers. It returns 2–3 options.
  4. Traffic effect. Each option also shows the same trip at a quiet hour, so you see how much traffic adds.
  5. Live layer. A held-out day is replayed as a live feed. Current road speeds adjust estimates, and live bus positions predict the next bus.

Accuracy

Everything is built on 1–24 October 2026. 25–31 October is held out and never used for building or tuning. "Truth" is always the real GPS bus rides; Google Maps is a second reference.

Example, Mahipalpur → Andheria More at 18:30: Google 24 min, ours 36 min, real buses 42 min. Google tends to underestimate Delhi bus times at peak hour.

Known limits and what's next

Waiting at the stop is the weak part. Door to door, including waits, the first version plans about 20 minutes too short, because buses bunch and real waits are longer than "half the gap between buses". A new wait model brings the door-to-door error from 26.6% to about 19% on validation days; its final held-out test is in progress.

Also next: a calibrated time range ("usually 40 min, up to 48"), holidays as their own day type, and a larger Google Maps comparison including transfers.

Try the API

The planner needs the October GPS-derived model tables, so it runs locally rather than on this page. With the code and tables in place:

planner\run.bat
# then open http://127.0.0.1:8000  (map)  or  http://127.0.0.1:8000/docs  (API)
GET /journey?from=Kashmere Gate&to=Nehru Place&depart=2026-10-27T08:30
