Newest entries at the bottom. Times are IST, 2026-11-24.

## Decisions

- **Deterministic model, not ML.** Median stop-to-stop times by (route segment, hour, weekday/weekend) from GPS. Wait = half the observed headway (capped at 20 min). ML on residuals is optional later.
- **Train on 1–24 Oct, hold out 25–31 Oct** for validation.
- **Processing stays on Windows.** Raw GPS never leaves `E:\hackathon-data`; only aggregated tables move.
- **Planner is frequency-based RAPTOR**, not timetable-based, because buses don't follow the schedule closely.
- **Windows repo `E:\hackathon-project` is the source of truth** for code and docs.
