Owner: ml. Branch v2/ml. Status: done (numbers below); the decision is for humans.

## What changed
- `model/v3/v2/scripts/build_rides_v2.py`: copy of `build_rides.py` that also writes `base_blend<S>` for every S in env
  `BLENDS` (default 300,450,600,900,1200), and with `HOLIDAY_DT=1` pools 2 and 20 Oct with weekends (section 4).
  Outputs go to `E:\hackathon-work\v2\ml`. Run with lookup days 1-18 and ride days 19-24 it reproduces exactly the
  300k fold-4 rides (same seed; check in `blend_select.json`).
- `model/v3/v2/scripts/blend_select.py`: median APE / within ±20% / bias, overall and by ride-length bucket (actual),
  for median, mean and every blend.
- `model/v3/v2/scripts/band_v2.py` (first part): the 3 xgb_final.py configs, trained on days 1-18, tree count checked
  every 10 trees (10..1000) on days 19-24 by median APE. Curve in `E:\hackathon-work\v2\ml\t04_tree_curve.csv`.
- No flag: nothing is served differently. BLEND_S stays an env var read by planner/ttmodel.py (default 600, data/planner own it).

## Validation (300k rides, days 19-24, tables from days 1-18). Median APE %, by actual ride length
| Base | all | within ±20% | <10 | 10-30 | 30-60 | >60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| median (v1 day types) | 13.05 | 66.5 | 13.66 | 11.82 | 13.16 | 16.59 |
| blend 300 | 13.45 | 65.4 | 14.00 | 12.73 | 13.06 | 13.93 |
| blend 600 (v1 default) | 13.21 | 66.0 | 13.66 | 12.50 | 13.12 | 14.07 |
| blend 900 | 13.10 | 66.2 | 13.66 | 12.19 | 13.18 | 14.24 |
| **blend 1200** | **13.08** | 66.3 | 13.66 | 12.02 | 13.34 | 14.45 |
| mean | 14.77 | 61.6 | 16.57 | 13.18 | 13.09 | 13.75 |

With the section-4 holiday day type (2 and 20 Oct pooled with weekends, `val19_24_hol`): median 12.42, blend 300 12.92,
450 12.76, 600 12.67, 900 12.55, **1200 12.49**, mean 14.26. The holiday day type alone cuts base error 13.05 → 12.42.

**Choice under the card's rule (best of {300..1200} on 19-24): BLEND_S = 1200**, under both day-type schemes.
Caveats for the human decision:
1. Plain median (no blend) is better overall than every blend on 19-24 (13.05 vs 13.08; 12.42 vs 12.49); the blend only
   wins on rides > 60 min (16.6 → 13.9-14.5). Error falls monotonically with S, so 1200 is a grid edge.
2. Sensitivity (diagnostic, not used to choose): on days 21-24 only (no Dussehra / its eve) blend 600 is best (12.47 vs
   1200 12.53, median 12.72). On days 19-20 the larger S wins (buses faster during Dussehra). So the 1200 pick is driven
   by the holiday days; with holiday tables it is still 1200 but the margin over 900 is 0.07 pt.

## Tree count every 10 trees (L1 XGBoost, train 1-18, validate 19-24)
| Config | best rounds (every 10) | val median APE | every-100 pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| depth 10, lr 0.03, mcw 50 (v1 served config) | **10** | 13.46 | 100 → 13.76 (= v1 meta 13.76) |
| depth 9, lr 0.05, mcw 100 | 10 | 13.48 | |
| depth 7, lr 0.05, mcw 200 | 10 | 13.48 | |
Sum of medians on the same rides: 13.05. **On validation, every XGBoost model is worse than no model, and more trees
make it worse.** v1 picked 100 because 100 was the smallest value it checked. Cause (diagnosed): the target
log(actual/base) has strong day-level shifts: median +0.07..+0.09 on days 7-17 weekdays, but -0.094 on 20 Oct
(Dussehra) and -0.014 on the 19th; the model learns the training-days intercept and the `weekend` feature does not know
holidays. The v1 test gain (12.28 vs 12.69) is on 25-31 Oct, which has no holiday.
Refit on 1-24 with 10 trees saved as `E:\hackathon-work\v2\ml\xgb_final_r10.json` (NOT served, reference only).
