# T0.1 Passing index + stop visits (with amendment A1) · data

Status: done for days 01-31. Gate decisions are for humans.

## What changed
- `model/scripts/stop_visits.py DD` (new): raw SQLite pings (opened read-only, `mode=ro`) to per-(stop, route) visit times.
  A visit is a run of points of one vehicle carrying route_id R within 60 m of a stop on R's pattern
  (`E:\hackathon-work\route_patterns.csv`, GTFS stops). A new visit starts after a gap of more than 120 s.
  Vectorised with a scipy cKDTree (k=8, `distance_upper_bound=60`) and a pattern-membership filter.
  **Densification (my addition):** pings come every 10-15 s, so a fast bus can cross a 60 m circle without
  pinging inside it. Points are inserted every 40 m on the straight line between consecutive pings of the same
  vehicle and route (gap at most 300 s, jump at most 2.5 km), with linear time. Turn it off with `VISITS_DENSIFY=0`.
  Each output carries `params=[60,120,40,300]`. The file is written to a temporary name and then renamed.
- `model/scripts/passing_index.py DD[,DD..]` (new): contract 1, built from `pass_DD.npz`. Arrays are sorted by
  (stop, t) and have a per-stop index. Usable share per route = map-matched trips / raw (vehicle, trip_id) trips.
- `model/scripts/run_visits.py N days` (new): runs the visits script for several days, N at a time.
- Outputs in `E:\hackathon-work\v2\data\`: `stop_visits_DD.npz`, `route_trips_DD.csv` and `passing_index_DD.npz` for
  DD = 01..31. `README.txt` there documents every array.

## Validation (no tuning on 25-31; 27 Oct was used only to compare visit coverage across settings, against map-matched passings, not against anything the test scores)
- Load time: stop_visits and passing_index each load in under 0.1 s per day (target: under 5 s).
- Share of map-matched passings that have a visit at the same route and stop within ±120 s:
  raw pings only 79.3% (day 20), densify ≤120 s 89.3%, densify ≤300 s 91.5% (day 27, 4M-row sample);
  final files: 92.4% (day 21) and 92.5% (day 22). Remaining misses: stops whose GTFS coordinates are more than 60 m
  from the road the buses use, and longer ping gaps.
- Spot check, day 22, buses per hour (visits | map-matched passings), hours 6/8/10/13/17/19/22:
  Badarpur Border (T), 37 routes: 112/216/188/156/202/167/140 | 66/97/88/71/83/54/72;
  Azadpur Terminal, 109 routes: 73/210/199/116/160/152/87 | 30/122/136/81/99/110/56;
  EDM Mall / Gazipur Depot, 58 routes: 95/158/175/108/138/144/102 | 65/112/115/88/98/85/81.
  Peaks fall at 08-10 and 17-19 with a midday dip, and visits are 1.3-2.4x the passings, as expected when only
  about 70% of trips map-match.
- Size: about 1.5-1.6 M visits per day, versus 1.25-1.66 M passings per day.
- Usable share (matched/raw trips, all routes): 0.69-0.76 on 01-21 Oct; 0.59-0.69 on 22-31 Oct.

## Open issues
- Raw trip_id counts per day jump from about 87k to about 115k starting 22 Oct (the feed seems to split trips more
  finely), so usable_share drops on 22-31 for reasons not about matching. Use it only as a diagnostic (A1).
- `veh` in passing_index (the pass-file vehicle index) and `veh` in stop_visits (an index into `veh_names`) use
  different encodings. Match on (route, stop, time), not on vehicle.
- At terminals `t` is the start of the layover. Use `t_last` (about the departure) for boarding there.
- Buses parked near a stop with a route_id set show up as long visits (large `t_last - t`).
- CPU: to get 19-31 out first, up to 6-7 visit jobs ran at once for about 20 min (more than the ~4 budget).
  The first, undensified files for 19-26 were rebuilt in place.
