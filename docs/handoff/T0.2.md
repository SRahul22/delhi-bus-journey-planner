# T0.2 Realized door-to-door journeys (eval) -- done for val; test file see status

## What changed
- `eval/realized_journeys.py --set val|test` (contract 6, with A1). Plans in-process with the v1 planner
  (`planner/router.py Planner`, default flags, V3_MODE=xgb, network `E:\hackathon-project\planner\cache\network.pkl`,
  MODEL_DIR=E:\hackathon-project\model), takes the recommended option (options[0]) and replays it on the day's
  real buses from the data agent's `stop_visits_DD.npz`:
  - walks as planned (1.2 m/s, detour 1.3);
  - boarding: first visit of the planned route_id at the boarding stop with t_last >= rider arrival, whose
    vehicle next reaches the alighting stop within 3 h and does not come back to the boarding stop first
    (rejects opposite-direction buses that pass within 60 m); depart = visit t_last; arrive = same vehicle's first
    point within 60 m of the alighting stop; repeat per transfer; egress walk added;
  - unscored when a leg has no such bus within 2 h (reason column). usable_share is a diagnostic column only (A1).
- Sets: 60 ticket pairs at their clock time on every non-holiday weekday (val: 19, 21, 22, 23 Oct; 20 Oct =
  Dussehra), plus 5,000 random OD-times (seed 2026, planner-served stops 2-20 km apart, 06:00-22:00, day uniform).
- Plans are cached (`plans_<set>.pkl`); `--plans-from-csv` rebuilds plans from an earlier csv (checked exact on
  240/240 itineraries), so a re-run on rebuilt stop visits takes seconds instead of ~25 min.
- `family_*` columns (v2 of the script): same replay but any bus of the route number (UP/DOWN pair). Turned out
  to change almost nothing because stop visits are only recorded for stops on the label route's own pattern.

## Validation results (val, days 19-24; `docs/results/v2_2026-11-24_val.md`)
- 5,240 OD-times (240 pair-days + 5,000 random). Scored: pairs 214/240 (89.2%), random 2,769/5,000 (55.4%).
- **Ticket pairs scored on >= 3 days: 54 of 60 (90%), transfer pairs 8 of 10.** (Card target: >= 90% of the 60
  pairs; judged by humans.) Never scored on 3 days: P08 (airport, route 2582 has 1 visit at the board stop all day),
  P32, P50 (route 1705: one bus all day at the board stop), P57, P60 (route 1484 runs at night only in GPS), and
  P53/P39 second legs (no bus within 2 h).
- Unscored reasons (all): no_visit_after_arrival 954, no_visits_board 569, no_bus_2h 461, no_visits_alight 267,
  walk_only 6. The random set is scored much less often than pairs: random ODs often get routes with few GPS-tracked
  buses (min usable_share median 0.65).
- Plan vs realized door to door (scored, n = 2,983): median error 26.6%, within +-20% 38.4%, median bias -1,260 s.
  Direct 24.5% (n 1,131), transfer 27.9% (n 1,852).
- **In-vehicle time is right; waits are not.** Median ride bias -6 s (pairs +2 s). First-wait median bias -462 s;
  mean real first wait 1,410 s vs planned 428 s (median 845 vs 326 s); 10% of scored waits exceed 1 h. Transfers
  add a further -598 s median bias. Midday service gaps of 1-2.5 h at a stop are common in the GPS
  (e.g. P05 route 2097 stop 3207: no bus 12:05-14:29 on 19 Oct).
- v1 door-to-door band (`duration_p85_s`): share under p50/p85/p90 = 18.7% / 50.9% / 57.7% (G2 asks 47-53 / 82-88 /
  87-93). The >60 min bucket is where it breaks (coverage 42.5%, bias -1,896 s); 30-60 min is fine (15.5% error,
  bias -96 s, coverage 80%).
- Re-realized after the data agent's final dense visits (params 60/120/40/300): same scored set, max change 281 s.

## Caveats (read before using the waits)
- A bus is only seen if its GPS carries the planned route_id and passes within 60 m. Buses with a missing or wrong
  route_id are invisible, so real waits are an upper bound; scoring is conditional on a bus within 2 h (lower-bound
  effect on long gaps). Both are diagnostics for T2.1, not reasons to drop routes (A1).
- `family_*` columns (UP/DOWN pair allowed) score 57.2% vs 56.9%: label mismatch is not the main cause.
- Each OD is planned once at its depart time with the v1 planner; the planner treats 20 Oct (holiday) as a weekday.
